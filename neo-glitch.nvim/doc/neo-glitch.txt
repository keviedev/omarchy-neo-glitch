                   NEO GLITCH - A CYBERPUNK COLORSCHEME FOR NEOVIM

Matches the Omarchy neo-glitch theme (github.com/keviedev/omarchy-neo-glitch).

Install: >
    { dir = "~/path/to/omarchy-neo-glitch/neo-glitch.nvim" }
    vim.cmd.colorscheme("neo-glitch")

Palette: void black #0a0612 · neon green #00ff9f · cyan #00e5ff ·
magenta #ea00d9 · red #ff2a6d · yellow #f9f871 · orange #ff9e4f ·
purple selection #2a1040.
